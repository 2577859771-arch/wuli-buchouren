# Public open-source components

These components are installed at pinned versions and served locally by the Site.
The build copies their original license notices into `/vendor/`.

| Component | Public source | Version | License | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| KaTeX | https://github.com/KaTeX/KaTeX | 0.19.0 | MIT | Safe mathematical typesetting |
| Marked | https://github.com/markedjs/marked | 18.0.14 | MIT | Answer headings, steps and tables |
| DOMPurify | https://github.com/cure53/DOMPurify | 3.4.16 | Apache-2.0 or MPL-2.0 | Sanitize model-generated Markdown output |

No whole third-party physics site or exercise dataset has been copied. Physics
models and teaching diagrams remain the Site's existing implementation.
